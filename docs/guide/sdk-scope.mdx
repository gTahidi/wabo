---
title: SDK Scope
---

Wabo covers messaging, business account and phone-number management, templates,
media, Flows and incoming webhook events. See the [package reference](/api-reference/wabo)
for the entry point and the [README](https://github.com/gTahidi/wabo#usage) for a Go example.

Support depends on the selected Graph version and the business account's permissions
and eligibility. Complete BSUID routing, pricing analytics and Marketing API campaign
management are not currently implemented. See the
[implementation report](https://github.com/gTahidi/wabo/blob/master/docs/guide/improvement-execution.md)
for remaining work.
