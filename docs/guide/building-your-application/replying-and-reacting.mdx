---
title: Replying and Reacting
---

Use `messaging.Message.Reply(message, recipientPhoneNumber, incomingMessageID)`
to send a reply. `ReplyContext` accepts a context for cancellation and deadlines.
Check the returned error.

To react, construct a reaction with `components.NewReactionMessage`, supplying
`MessageId` and `Emoji` in `components.ReactionMessageParams`, then send it through
the message manager. See the [component reference](/api-reference/pkg/components)
and [message manager reference](/api-reference/manager).
